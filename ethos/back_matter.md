# About the Author

Ashish Tyagi is a founder, software developer, and longtime reader who cares about the people inside the systems we build. He holds master's degrees in mathematics, computer science, and business, lives in the United States, and writes about the moral questions builders and leaders face when the easier path is in plain view. *Ethos: The Promise* is his debut novel.
