**Author's Note**

*Ethos: The Promise* is a story about the questions every leader eventually faces — about duty, action, detachment, and the cost of doing what is right when the easier path is in plain view. Aaron's eighteen-hour ordeal in the boardroom is a compressed version of a struggle most of us live across years: the struggle to keep building something true in a world that rewards extraction over creation.

The lessons that surface in these pages are not new. They have been written before, in many forms, in many languages, in many traditions. What this novel hopes to offer is the reminder that those lessons still apply — even in glass towers, even at 9 AM, even when the spreadsheet is asking you to choose.
