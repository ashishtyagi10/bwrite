# Engagement tactics

1. **Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours.** LinkedIn's algorithm boosts posts with quick engagement.
2. **Don't put the link in the post itself** — for maximum LinkedIn reach, the first version of a post should put the link in the FIRST COMMENT, not the post body. LinkedIn de-prioritizes posts that send users off-platform. So: write the post without the link, then add `https://books2read.com/u/bP228d` as your own first reply.
3. **Ask one specific person to comment** in the first 30 minutes. Even one engaged comment from a connected human signals quality to the algorithm.
4. **Tag 2-3 relevant people** if they'd genuinely want to read it. Don't tag-spam — LinkedIn punishes that. But 2-3 thoughtful tags work.
5. **Reshare your top-performing post 4-6 weeks later** with a small update ("A month in — here's what I've learned"). LinkedIn shows reshared posts to new audiences.
