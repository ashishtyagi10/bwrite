## KDP categories (pick 2)

KDP lets you choose two browse categories. These are the highest-ROI for your book:

**Primary:** Fiction › Literary Fiction
**Secondary:** Fiction › Thrillers & Suspense › Financial

Alternates if either is overcrowded the day you upload:
- Fiction › Business & Workplace

## Pricing recommendation

| Format | Launch price | Standard price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ebook (Kindle, Apple, Google, Kobo) | $0.99 | $3.99 | Launch low to drive reviews; raise after ~20 reviews |
| Paperback (KDP, IngramSpark) | $12.99 | $12.99 | Steady — paperback launches don't need discounting |
| Hardcover (optional, KDP) | $24.99 | $24.99 | Only if you want a premium tier |

Royalty math:
- Ebook at $3.99 → ~$2.79 royalty per sale (KDP 70%)
- Ebook at $0.99 → ~$0.34 royalty per sale (KDP 35%, since $0.99 is below the $2.99 threshold for 70%)
- Paperback at $12.99 → ~$3.30 royalty per sale after printing cost

## Where to publish (recommendation)

**Wide distribution, not Amazon-exclusive.** Your book has literary appeal — Apple Books readers buy more literary fiction than Kindle readers, and locking yourself out of Apple/Google for the 90 days KDP Select demands is a bad trade for a debut.

Recommended platform list:
1. **Amazon KDP** (direct upload) — paperback + ebook
2. **Apple Books for Authors** (direct upload) — ebook, supports promo codes
3. **Google Play Books Partner Center** (direct upload) — ebook, supports discount codes
4. **Draft2Digital** (aggregator, free, takes 10%) — pushes to Kobo, Barnes & Noble Press, Tolino, Vivlio, Borrow Box, Libby/OverDrive (libraries), Bibliotheca
5. **IngramSpark** (optional, $0 setup fee 2024+, $1.49 per upload revision) — gets your paperback into independent bookstores and libraries worldwide. Worth it if you want bookstore presence.

Skip these unless you have a specific reason:
- Smashwords (Draft2Digital acquired them; D2D covers their reach)
- Lulu (worse royalties than KDP for most authors)
- BookBaby (charges upfront; you can do the same things free)

## Free copies / promo codes by platform

| Platform | Mechanism | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon KDP | Cannot issue codes. Use 5 "Free Book Promotion" days (KDP Select only — skip), or set the price to $0.99 universally. Best option for free copies: just send the .epub directly. | n/a |
| Apple Books | Promo codes (free downloads). | Up to **250 codes per book per quarter** — generate from iTunes Connect |
| Google Play | Discount codes (% off or free). | Variable; documented in Play Books Partner Center |
| Kobo | Cannot issue codes for individual users. Use temporary price drops. | n/a |
| All platforms | Send the .epub or .docx file directly | unlimited (your file) |

For ARC distribution (reviewers in exchange for honest reviews), the cleanest path is sending the .epub directly via email or **BookSprout** (free tier supports 25 active reviewers).
