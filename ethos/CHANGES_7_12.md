# Changes to Chapters 7–12

## Chapter 7
Cut the elevator-descent philosophy from roughly 3,500 words to about 1,800. Kept the floor-ticking-down structure as the chapter's forward motion, but broke Chris's long blocks with Aaron's reactions, his fear of facing Damian, and his memory of Sarah in the photographs along the wall. Removed roughly two-thirds of the "lower nature vs higher nature" reiteration and the "blinded by the fire" sequence. Replaced the closing bromide ("Everything else is just details") with a concrete sensory cue: the second elevator opening on 50, the boardroom door's brushed steel cold under Aaron's hand, Damian's muffled voice on the other side. Reduced "consciousness" substantially and trimmed "architecture" to "design / system / scaffolding" where it sounded affected. Built the floor map into the geography: 50 for the boardroom (reached by a short second elevator from the lobby).

## Chapter 8
Light line edits only, per the brief. Strengthened the EU investigation reveal with a specific case reference (EDPB 2025/EDPB/0417, Irish DPC lead authority, Brussels), which gets picked up again in ch9 and remains available for ch16's payoff. Fixed "Telegraph Avenue in Berkeley" basement to the canonical damp Cambridge basement near MIT. Fixed Marcus's "Aaron's dorm room at Berkeley" memory to the kitchen table of the Cambridge apartment. Collapsed the long, double-stated IPO Memory passage so the textures-and-temperature reverie is one paragraph instead of four, and removed the duplicate one-paragraph IPO recap that opened "Entering the Field." Sharpened the chapter close: cut the post-cliffhanger philosophizing ("In that moment, standing in the hallway… he was already, in some sense, victorious") and ended on Chris's single-word acknowledgment and the corridor going quiet behind Aaron.

## Chapter 9
Major structural rewrite. Removed the final unanimous 7–0 vote at line 149 entirely. Removed Damian's exit "you'll regret this" speech, the post-vote dialogue between Marcus and the others, and the closing "he had done what he came to do" beat — all of those resolved the plot prematurely. The chapter now ends with Dalton calling for a procedural recess (not a substantive vote) to allow counsel to review the EDPB filing, the standstill agreement, and the legal feasibility of the API pivot. The procedural motion carries with the buyout offer still on the table; nothing about the underlying takeover is decided. Damian walks out (per the editorial bible's choice to anchor his physical exit at ch9 procedurally rather than ch14's defeat). Aaron's "seventeen years old" line (line 105) changed to "sixteen." Kept Aaron's API pivot pitch intact — it remains the strongest business content in the chapter. The chapter now ends with "the fight had just begun" rather than the previous false-victory note.

## Chapter 10
Opens cleanly from ch9's recess (no longer requires a five-hour time-jump to be explained). Cut Chris's Elena's-mother monologue from roughly 1,500 words to about 450, rendered as Aaron and Chris in back-and-forth: Aaron asks, Chris answers in short beats, Aaron pushes back, Chris answers again. Elena's backstory now lands as the canonical version: born in Bangladesh, brother died at thirteen of an undiagnosed congenital heart defect, family emigrated when Elena was eight, mother trained in Dhaka as a cardiac surgeon, credentials didn't transfer, three jobs in Iowa. This unifies the ch10 and ch12 versions. Kept Aaron's "you asked me earlier if I could guarantee these numbers" speech intact — it is the strongest beat in the chapter. Reframed the chapter close so the room recesses again for the reconsideration motion at 4:15 (no premature victory framing); Sarah catches Aaron in the corridor to set up ch11's hand-off into ch12.

## Chapter 11
Preserved the Universal Form visualization as the chapter's cinematic centerpiece. Cut roughly a third of the red-nodes/blue-nodes descriptions — once each color and process is established, the prose now trusts the reader and stops re-explaining. Kept the visceral horror of Sarah recognizing the algorithm that killed her father in the red layer. Removed the long post-visualization interior monologues attributed to Patricia Oakes, William Chen, and Thomas Brennan (their granddaughter, the daughter at UCSF, his own mortality) — those four pages were telling, not showing. Replaced the chapter's resolution beat: the visualization triggers Patricia Oakes to move that the API pivot enter operative position pending counsel review and a substantive vote, Marcus seconds, and Dalton schedules the substantive vote for the next session. Damian leaves visibly shaken but not defeated. Closes with Sarah catching Aaron's sleeve in the corridor and asking to see him in his office — direct setup for ch12's redemption scene.

## Chapter 12
Major rewrite to enact Sarah's redemption in a scene rather than narrating it. Sarah arrives at Aaron's office, places a manila folder of her communications with Damian on the desk, and speaks for herself across eight discrete lines of dialogue: she admits to authenticating the vulnerability from her own workstation, names her financial fear ($12M after-tax against her mother's $6K/month nursing-home waitlist), takes responsibility, asks Aaron to read her disclosure into the board record on her behalf so the cost falls on him, and commits to going inside the acquisition as an informant if the vote fails. Reconciled Elena's backstory to match the ch10 canonical version (born in Bangladesh, brother died at thirteen, family emigrated when Elena was eight, mother was a Dhaka-trained cardiac surgeon working three jobs in Iowa). Deleted the duplicate "Elena went to UC Berkeley on full scholarship" paragraph that previously appeared at both line 119 and line 127 (now appears once, integrated into the corridor walk between Aaron and Chris). Removed the long "Devotion Transforms the Room" interior-architecture passage. Aaron's confrontation of Sarah's father story is now told once (in his floor speech at the evening session) and not retold by Chris. The chapter closes by carrying the substitution motion (API pivot now in operative position) and scheduling the substantive vote for Thursday at 10 a.m. — direct procedural setup for ch13's reconsideration session. The substantive vote itself is not taken in ch12; it remains the 7–6 outcome ch14 will deliver.
